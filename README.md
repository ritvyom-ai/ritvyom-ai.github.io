## About Yashora Reel Generator

**Yashora Reel Generator** turns a simple text idea or script into a complete, ready-to-share video. Created under **RITVYOM** by **Surya Dev Nishad**, it is designed for **text-to-video creation**, **faceless YouTube channels**, **students**, **content creators**, **marketers**, **educators**, and **everyday storytellers** who want to go from an idea to a finished video smoothly.

## How It Turns Text into a Video

1. **Start from Create Reel:** Open the home screen and tap **Create Reel** to write your own script, paste text, or generate a script using AI.
2. **Generate Voiceovers:** Connect your **Gemini** or **ElevenLabs** API key to generate natural, expressive narration.
3. **Add Visuals & Captions:** Pull matching visuals from integrated media sources and add clear captions or subtitles.
4. **Render & Export Locally:** Export finished videos directly on your device in your preferred aspect ratio, resolution, and watermark setting.
