Frequently Asked Questions

How can I create a complete video using only text?

In Yashora Reel Generator (by Surya Dev Nishad / RITVYOM), simply enter or AI-generate your text script, generate a voiceover with your Gemini or ElevenLabs API key, fetch visuals from integrated media sources, and export the finished video directly on your phone.

Can I remove the watermark or add my own social media handle?

Yes. Yashora Reel Generator lets you export videos with no watermark at all, or add your own custom social media handle as a watermark.

What video formats and resolutions are supported?

You can export in 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, and 4:5 aspect ratios across 360p, 720p, 1080p, and 2K resolutions.
