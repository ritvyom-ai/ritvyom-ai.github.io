Common Questions About Text-to-Video Creation

How can I make a video from text or a story on Android?

With Yashora Reel Generator (by Surya Dev Nishad / RITVYOM), you can write, paste, or AI-generate your text or story, generate voiceovers using your Gemini or ElevenLabs API key, fetch visuals from integrated media sources, and export the video directly on your device.

Can it be used for faceless YouTube channels and student projects?

Yes. Faceless channel creators can turn scripts into narrated videos with custom social handle watermarks or watermark-free export, while students can turn topics and notes into visual explainers.

Which aspect ratios and video qualities are supported?

You can export in 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, and 4:5 formats across 360p, 720p, 1080p, and 2K resolutions.
